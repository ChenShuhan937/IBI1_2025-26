Practical 9 Reflection
This practical was about building stochastic SIR models to simulate infection spread. 
The basic SIR model was straightforward after understanding random choice and population updates. 
The vaccination part helped me understand herd immunity intuitively. 
The spatial 2D model was more challenging, especially handling neighbors and state updates. 
I learned how to turn real-world problems into probabilistic simulations and use numpy for random processes and grid operations. 
The most important thing I learned is how stochastic models behave differently each run and how parameters like beta and gamma shape the outbreak.
